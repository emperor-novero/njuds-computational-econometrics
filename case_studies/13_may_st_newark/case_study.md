  $$\text{Lower Tolerance Limit} = R \times 0.85 = 0.4537 \times 0.85 = \mathbf{38.56\%}$$
  $$\text{Upper Tolerance Limit} = R \times 1.15 = 0.4537 \times 1.15 = \mathbf{52.18\%}$$
